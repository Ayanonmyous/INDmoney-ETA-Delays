What changed?

The problem

When a deposit misses its ETA, the supplied evidence suggests users may not know the next step; the exact current transaction screen remains unverified.

The change

A dashboard alert leads to a sample tracker with a plain-language delay message, last known update, status timeline and a prefilled support request.

Success metric

−20%

Target reduction in support requests per delayed transfer within eight weeks. Current baseline is unknown and must be measured.

Trade-off

Making delays more visible may expose operational problems, but gives users a clearer next step without promising faster transfers.

The screens beyond the supplied dashboard/account-setup references are proposed concepts. Transaction and support data are simulated locally.
